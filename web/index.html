hevm in the browser

The hevm symbolic EVM, the bitwuzla SMT solver and the Solidity compiler, all compiled to WebAssembly. Nothing leaves your machine.

Source contracts inheriting Test from hevm/Test.sol are tests; hevm checks their prove_ functions for all inputs

Prove a contract on the Solidity tab, then pick Replay in debugger on a counterexample, or Debug symbolically on any function.
